# pinterest

- **Start:** https://www.pinterest.com/pin-builder/
- **Signed in when:** the Pin builder opens (logged out: the home page)
- **Kit:** Articles page -> Copy text (title, description, tracked link)
- **Steps:**
  1. Upload the article cover PNG.
  2. Title = first line; description = the rest; destination link = the tracked link from the kit.
  3. Pick a board (create 'NerdIT articles' once).
- **Canonical / original URL:** Not needed.
- **Images:** The cover is the Pin.
- **Publish:** Publish. At most one Pin per day (runlog enforces it).
- **Post URL:** https://www.pinterest.com/pin/<id>/
- **Verified:** no
