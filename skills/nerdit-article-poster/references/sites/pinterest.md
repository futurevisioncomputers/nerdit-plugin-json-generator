# pinterest

- **Start:** https://www.pinterest.com/pin-builder/ (redirects to in.pinterest.com; account Nerdit.in, business)
- **Signed in when:** the Pin builder opens (logged out: the home page)
- **Kit:** title, description, tracked link: link = article URL +
  `?utm_source=pinterest&utm_medium=share&utm_campaign=<slug>`.
- **Steps:**
  1. Upload the article cover PNG to the builder's hidden file input ('File upload'). Pinterest warns it is under
     1500 px tall; that is fine.
  2. 'Add your title' = the article title; 'Tell everyone what your Pin is about' = the description; 'Add alt text'
     = one sentence saying what the cover shows; 'Add a destination link' = the tracked link.
  3. Board (drop-down next to Publish) = **NerdIT articles** (made 2026-11-24).
- **Canonical / original URL:** Not needed.
- **Images:** The cover is the Pin.
- **Publish:** Publish -> 'You created a Pin!'. At most one Pin per day (runlog enforces it).
- **Post URL:** the 'See your Pin' link: https://www.pinterest.com/pin/<id>/ (save it in this www form).
- **Verified:** yes, 2026-11-24
