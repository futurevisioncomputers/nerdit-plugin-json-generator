# quora

- **Start:** the Open link on the Articles page (a Quora search for the title)
- **Signed in when:** your avatar at top right
- **Kit:** Articles page -> Copy text (answer starter + tracked link)
- **Steps:**
  1. Quora: pick a question the article really answers (recent, with views). Reddit: pick a subreddit and read its rules on self-promotion first; skip it if links are not allowed.
  2. Rewrite the starter into a real answer in plain words (the link goes at the end).
  3. Show the owner the question/subreddit and the answer text; post only after their 'yes' for this answer.
- **Canonical / original URL:** Not needed.
- **Images:** None.
- **Publish:** Quora: Answer -> Post. Reddit: Create post -> Post. At most one per day (runlog enforces it).
- **Post URL:** The answer or post URL from its share menu.
- **Verified:** no
