# blogger

- **Start:** https://www.blogger.com/
- **Signed in when:** your blog's Posts list
- **Kit:** Articles page -> Copy text (opening + link)
- **Steps:**
  1. New post -> paste the title, then the body in Compose view.
- **Canonical / original URL:** None.
- **Images:** Upload images where their lines are (Insert image -> Upload).
- **Publish:** Add 3-5 labels from the tags -> Publish -> Confirm.
- **Post URL:** https://<blog>.blogspot.com/<yyyy>/<mm>/<slug>.html
- **Verified:** no
