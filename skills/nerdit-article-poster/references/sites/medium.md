# medium

- **Start:** https://medium.com/p/import
- **Signed in when:** avatar at top right, no 'Sign in'
- **Kit:** Articles page -> Copy link (the article URL)
- **Steps:**
  1. Paste the article URL into the box -> Import (if 'Import failed', open the nerdit.in article once to wake it, then retry).
  2. 'See your story' -> the draft opens. If the first image is an old or wrong cover, click it and press Delete, then Backspace the empty line.
  3. Check the text and code blocks look right.
- **Canonical / original URL:** Automatic: Medium's import adds 'Originally published at nerdit.in' and the canonical link.
- **Images:** Imported from the page (body images come along).
- **Publish:** Publish (green, top right) -> add up to 5 topics from the article tags -> Publish now.
- **Post URL:** The address bar after publishing: https://medium.com/@<user>/<slug>-<id>.
