# dailydev

- **Start:** https://app.daily.dev/squads
- **Signed in when:** your avatar at top right
- **Kit:** Articles page -> Copy text (title, note, tracked link)
- **Steps:**
  1. Join a Squad that fits the article (Python, AI, web dev) -> New post -> Share a link.
  2. Paste the tracked link as the link and the rest as the commentary.
- **Canonical / original URL:** Not needed.
- **Images:** None (daily.dev shows the article's cover from its page).
- **Publish:** Post.
- **Post URL:** The post URL in the address bar (https://app.daily.dev/posts/<id>).
- **Verified:** no
