# substack

- **Start:** https://substack.com/
- **Signed in when:** Dashboard link / your avatar
- **Kit:** Articles page -> Copy text (opening + link)
- **Steps:**
  1. Dashboard -> New post -> Text post.
  2. Paste the title, then the body.
- **Canonical / original URL:** None. The kit is only the opening and ends with 'Read the full article on NerdIT'.
- **Images:** Upload images where their lines are (pasted markdown images do not show).
- **Publish:** Continue -> choose web only (no email to subscribers unless the owner says so) -> Publish.
- **Post URL:** https://<pub>.substack.com/p/<slug>
- **Verified:** no
